abstract: Understanding the joint evolution of galaxies and halos is central to galaxy
  evolution and cosmology. Using Graph Neural Networks, I will show that galaxy properties
  can be predicted equally well from a halo’s spatial environment or its assembly
  history, across both hydrodynamic and semi-analytic simulations. We also measure
  the characteristic scale needed to constrain each parameter, and determine the physical
  reason for the value of these scales.
